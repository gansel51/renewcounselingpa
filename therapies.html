Focused Therapies

How I can help

Counseling isn't one-size-fits-all. I tailor our work to you, drawing on approaches that are proven to help.

Anxiety & Stress

When worry takes up more room than it should, it can affect your sleep, your relationships, and your ability to enjoy life. Together we'll understand what fuels your anxiety and build practical skills to calm your body and mind.

  • Generalized anxiety and constant worry
  • Panic attacks
  • Work and school stress, burnout

Depression

Depression can make everything feel heavy, even the things you used to love. We'll work at a pace that feels manageable to rebuild energy, hope, and a sense of connection.

  • Low mood, numbness, or loss of motivation
  • Negative self-talk
  • Seasonal and situational depression

Trauma & Healing

Painful experiences can leave lasting roots. In a safe, steady space, we'll gently work through what happened so it holds less power over your present.

  • Past and recent traumatic experiences
  • Childhood and relational trauma
  • Trauma-informed, client-led care

Life Transitions & Grief

Even welcome changes can be disorienting. Whether you're navigating loss, a new chapter, or a change in who you are, I'll help you find your footing.

  • Grief and loss
  • Career changes, moves, and new roles
  • Becoming a parent, empty nest, relationship changes
My toolkit

Approaches I use

I draw from evidence-based methods and adapt them to what fits you best.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Identify unhelpful thought patterns and practice new ways of thinking and responding.

Mindfulness-Based Approaches

Build present-moment awareness to ease stress and respond to difficult feelings with care.

Person-Centered Therapy

You are the expert on your own life. I offer empathy, honesty, and a space to explore.

You don't need to know exactly what's wrong to reach out. A free consultation is a good place to begin.
