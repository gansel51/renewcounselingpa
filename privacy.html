What this site collects

If you use the contact form, the site collects what you type: your name, email, phone number, and message. It does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and it does not run analytics.

How your message is handled

Form messages are delivered to me by email through a form service, Formspree. Email and web forms are not a fully secure way to share health information, so please keep your message brief and save personal details for our conversation.

How it's used

I use your information only to respond to you and to schedule a consultation. I do not sell or share it for marketing.

Other services on this site

  • Google Maps shows the office map on the Contact page. Google may collect basic technical information when the map loads.
  • Google Fonts provides the site's typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google.

This website and its contact form are not monitored for emergencies. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 or call 911.
